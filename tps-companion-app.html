Phase 1 — Win/Win (Stages 1–3)
Parties still believe mutual resolution is possible. Direct facilitated conversation. Your role: mediator.
Signals: tension, passive communication, avoided topics
Phase 2 — Win/Lose (Stages 4–6)
Parties solidified into positions. Structured intervention, possible admin involvement. Your role: authority.
Signals: ally recruitment, formal complaints, deliberate exclusion
Phase 3 — Lose/Lose (Stages 7–9)
Destructive — escalate above your rank. Your role: protective.
Signals: sabotage, threats, willing to destroy relationships

Each dysfunction builds on the one below. Absence of trust is the root of every dysfunctional team. You cannot fix accountability avoidance without first addressing the trust deficit. Always diagnose from the bottom up.
Assessment Application
In team building and culture scenarios: "Using Lencioni's model, I believe this crew is experiencing the second dysfunction — fear of conflict. That tells me the root issue is trust, not the surface-level disagreements I'm seeing."

Temporal Proximity Test
Discipline within close temporal proximity to protected activity (grievance filing, union organizing, safety reporting) presumptively appears retaliatory. Before submitting any discipline following protected activity: consult HR, document the timeline, ensure the discipline would have occurred regardless of the protected activity.
